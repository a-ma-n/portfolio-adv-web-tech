LivStory

Livstory is an Android application, which uses real-time background sound generation as the user speaks a story. Our AI algorithm, picks up sound real-time and plays the sound effects to make the story a great memory.

Learn More

Features:

  1. Uses lemmatization and Natural Language processing to process and play various sound intelligently based on the text spoken in real-time
  2. Includes a variety of sounds from different aspects of life: nature, animals as well as cartoon sounds
  3. Runs on an Azure hosted ML backend with Firebase powered audio delivery, enabling scalable, low-latency sound generation for mobile storytelling
